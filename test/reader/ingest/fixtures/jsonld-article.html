The Quiet Revolution in Type Design

For decades the tools of typeface design sat behind expensive, specialized software that few outside the profession ever touched. That is changing, and faster than most practitioners expected, as a new generation of open tools reaches maturity and finds an audience.

The shift is not merely technical. It reshapes who gets to participate, lowering the barrier for designers in places long excluded from the industry's traditional centers of power and patronage.

What follows is a look at the people and projects driving that change, and at the quiet institutional resistance still standing in their way as the old guard adjusts.
