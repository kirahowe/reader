On Writing Slowly

I have come to believe that the pace of writing matters as much as the volume, and that the rush to publish has quietly degraded the way we think on the page over the last decade of always-on platforms and their relentless feeds.

Slowness is not the same as laziness. It is a deliberate refusal to let the medium dictate the tempo of thought, a small act of resistance that pays back in clarity what it costs in speed and in reach.
